Memory

Memory, notes and reminders stay on this phone. Clearing the conversation keeps what Jarvis remembers about you.

AI provider

Pick which AI answers you. A green dot means you've added a key for it. Gemini, Groq and Mistral have free tiers; Groq is the fastest. Leave the model blank and Jarvis picks a quick one.

API keys

Add as many as you like. Keys are stored only on this phone and sent only to that provider. Tap “Get key” to open the provider's key page, copy the key, and paste it here.

Web search

When you ask about news, prices, scores or anything current, Jarvis looks it up. With a Gemini key it uses Google Search through Gemini (best). Otherwise it uses Google News and Wikipedia, or Tavily if you add a free key (1,000 searches a month).

Connection

Keys typed here are stored only on this phone. To keep them off the phone, put them in the Jarvis server's .env file and choose “Always via Jarvis server”.

Your commands

Teach Jarvis a phrase and what to do. Write each step on its own line in plain words, the way you'd say it: “turn off the flashlight”, “set an alarm for 6:30 am”, “open Spotify”. Use “say …” to make Jarvis speak and “wait 2 seconds” to pause. A step Jarvis can't do by itself is sent to the AI.

Or start from an example

Instant commands

These run on your phone in a split second without the AI, so they're fast, work when the AI is busy, and don't use your AI quota. Anything else — questions, messages, several steps at once — goes to the AI.

Notifications

Jarvis can read your notifications aloud, tell you what's important, and reply to messages. It looks at them only when you ask, never stores them, and sends them to the AI only when you ask the AI about them. Replies are always read back to you first.

Offturn it on so Jarvis can read and reply to notifications

Screen control

Like Android's Voice Access: Jarvis can go back, go home, scroll, tap buttons by their name, type into text boxes and read the screen aloud. The AI can also read the screen when you ask about it (“what does this message say?”). Jarvis only reads the screen when you ask.

Offturn it on to control apps by voice

Android 13 and newer: if it says Restricted setting, tap Open app info, then ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, and turn it on again.

Floating Jarvis

The bubble floats over every app and keeps running in the background. Drag it onto the ✕ at the bottom of the screen to close it.

Updates

Updates come from the project's GitHub releases. Jarvis downloads the new version and Android asks you to confirm the install. Your settings and memory are kept.
